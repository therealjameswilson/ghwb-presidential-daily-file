# GHWB Presidential Daily File

A chronological catalog of documents from the **George H.W. Bush Presidential Daily File (1989–1993)**, curated from LinkedIn posts by [James Graham Wilson](https://www.linkedin.com/in/james-graham-wilson/), Cold War & Nuclear-Policy Historian and editor of FRUS.

Each entry lists the date of the underlying document (where known), a brief description, and references the post in the author's LinkedIn activity feed. Items grouped by year, then chronologically within the year. Dates inferred from documents shown in the posts; some entries record only the month/year of the document where the exact date is not visible.

Source: <https://www.linkedin.com/in/james-graham-wilson/recent-activity/all/>

---

## 1989

- **1989-06-12** — Handwritten note from President Bush to "Helmut" (Chancellor Kohl): gifting a West Virginia coal elephant figurine for the Kohls' elephant-collecting assistant. *(The President letterhead.)*
- **1989-12-03** — Handwritten thank-you note from Bush to Mrs. Susan G. Baker, en route Malta → Brussels: "Mm-mm good on the bread… thanks for the delicious Hi-Cal." *(Malta Summit with Gorbachev; "carboloading on the way to meet Mikhail Gorbachev off the coast of Malta.")*

## 1990

- **1990-01 (late)** — President Bush flags for Dick Cheney and Colin Powell a call from a "Very Elderly Lady."
- **1990-03-10** — Handwritten note from Bush at Camp David to Larry Thomas (The Irvine Co.): "Loved those Kindergarten views on who's President — but I didn't know George + Abe personally." *(bcc Sununu, Fitzwater, Demarest.)*
- **1990-03-13** — Letter to Ms. Pam Shriver (PHS, Ltd., Lutherville, MD); bcc Joe Hagin, Jan Burmeister.
- **1990-07-18** — Marlin Fitzwater press briefing ("All right. California here we come."); "Poor Marlin!"
- **1990-10-19** — AP article (John Diamond) on "backward tapes" / hidden code-word claims in Bush Persian-Gulf speeches; Sen. Claiborne Pell and aide C.B. Scott Jones; the mystery word: "Simone." Bush's annotation: "Wierd indeed!"
- **1990-12-31** — President Bush's New Year's Eve letter to his children ("35 years ago…").
- **1990 (undated, defense procurement)** — Memo cataloging effects of B-2 Bomber program on Northrup sales/employment and A-12 Fighter on General Dynamics / McDonnell-Douglas.

## 1991

- **1991-01-27 (post-Super Bowl XXV)** — President Bush writes Whitney Houston after her rendition of the Star-Spangled Banner; addressed c/o Ben Bernstein, Triad, Los Angeles.
- **1991-02 (Camp David weekend)** — Daily Diary: Camp David weekend featuring "The Field" and "Silence of the Lambs"; tennis, Aspen Bathhouse, horseshoes; Mrs. Bush walks the entire Camp; ambiguity over "Robin Hood: Prince of Thieves" (Costner) vs. "Robin Hood" (Bergen/Thurman).
- **1991 (Desert Storm)** — Bush correspondence with Soviet Foreign Minister Bessmertnykh during Operation Desert Storm.
- **1991 (Desert Storm)** — Correspondence between Bush and Sen. Daniel Patrick Moynihan on the use of force and a U.S. role beyond the Cold War.
- **1991 (post-Gulf War)** — Bush ponders recruiting Sen. Chuck Robb to the Republican Party.
- **1991-03-26** — Letter from President Bush to George P. Shultz (Hoover Institution, Stanford): "thrilled with the end of the war… the real tough work lies ahead." *("Respectful — yet not overly warm — relationship between the two Georges.")*
- **1991-05-30** — Pool Report: Bush meets with Cypriot President George Vassiliou in Oval Office; Bush jokes about thyroid / Millie-Graves theory ("1 in 3 million chance").
- **1991 (summer)** — Bush commutes with Brent Scowcroft regarding thyroid issues.
- **1991-08** — "Friday Funnies" from the Bush files.
- **1991-09-16** — Note from Brent Scowcroft to President Bush nominating a young German minister, Angela Merkel, for a Scowcroft Award (falling asleep while meeting with the president).
- **1991-11** — President Bush's letter to Magic Johnson (following HIV announcement).
- **1991 (late)** — Bush trash-talks with the White House Pool.
- **1991-12** — Letter from Madame Chiang Kai-shek to President Bush.

## Cross-cutting threads

- **Scowcroft Awards** (falling asleep in meetings with the President) — recurring motif: Angela Merkel (1991), Barbara Bush via Mulroney (1992).
- **Millie-Graves theory** — Bush family thyroid (Graves' Disease) and Millie the Dog; surfaced in 1991 Vassiliou pool report and Scowcroft commute notes.
- **Pool Reports** — 1991 Vassiliou meeting; 1991 White House trash-talk; 1992 NH primary eve; Nov 3, 1992 election day.
- **Handwritten Bush correspondence** — Kohl (1989); Susan Baker (1989, Malta); Larry Thomas (1990, kindergarten); Whitney Houston (1991); Shultz (1991); Magic Johnson (1991); Reagan (1992).
