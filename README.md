# GHWB Presidential Daily File

A chronological catalog of documents from the **George H.W. Bush Presidential Daily File (1989–1993)**, curated from LinkedIn posts by [James Graham Wilson](https://www.linkedin.com/in/james-graham-wilson/), Cold War & Nuclear-Policy Historian and editor of FRUS.

Each entry lists the date of the underlying document, the LinkedIn post age (approximate), and a brief description.

---

## 1989

- **1989-06-12** — Handwritten note from President Bush to "Helmut" (Chancellor Kohl): gifting a West Virginia coal elephant figurine for the Kohls' elephant-collecting assistant. *(Camp David / The President letterhead.)*
- **1989-12-03** — Handwritten thank-you note from Bush to Mrs. Susan G. Baker, en route Malta → Brussels: "Mm-mm good on the bread… thanks for the delicious Hi-Cal." *(Malta Summit with Gorbachev.)*

## 1990

- **1990-03-10** — Handwritten note from Bush at Camp David to Larry Thomas (The Irvine Co.): "Loved those Kindergarten views on who's President — but I didn't know George + Abe personally." *(bcc Sununu, Fitzwater, Demarest.)*
- **1990-07-18** — Press Briefing by Marlin Fitzwater, The Briefing Room, 10:12 AM EDT: parental leave bill; Concerned Alliance of Responsible Employers.
- **1990-10-19** — AP wire by John Diamond: "Backward Tapes Reveal Code Word to Pell Aide" — reversed audio of Bush's Persian Gulf speeches allegedly contains code word "Simone"; C.B. Scott Jones / Sen. Claiborne Pell episode.

## 1991

- **1991-02-02** — Bush handwritten note to Whitney Houston (Camp David): thanking her for her "Super role" performing the Star Spangled Banner at Super Bowl XXV. *(bcc Sununu, Bridget Montagne.)*
- **1991-03-11** — Letter from George P. Shultz (Hoover Institution) to President Bush: post-White House dinner; Thatcher's Medal of Freedom event.
- **1991-03-26** — Letter from Bush to Shultz: post-Gulf War, "the real tough work lies ahead."
- **1991-05-30** — Pool Report: Bush meets Cypriot President George Vassiliou in the Oval Office; jokes about thyroid and Millie-Graves disease link.
- **1991-11-13** — Pool Report (St. Louis → Andrews): Bush's handwritten reply about the Houston Oilers and St. Louis Cardinals — "Defensively yours (s/George Bush) *weak, I admit it!!! Go Oilers!!"

## 1992

- **1992-02-15** — *Congressional Quarterly* clipping: Bush Approval Rating chart (1989–1992) — Panama invasion (Dec 1989), Iraq invades Kuwait (Aug 1990), war begins (Jan 16, 1991), Bush drops no-tax pledge (June 26).
- **1992-02-15** — Pool Report, Manchester, NH: Bush lunch at the Fireside Restaurant with Schwarzenegger, Sununu, Franco Columbu, Gov. Weld, John Stubbs; Roger Higgins voter quote.
- **1992-05-29** — *Washington Post* clipping ("Perot Advisers"): "Outsider Sifting Advice Inside Beltway"; Perot turning to veterans of Bush, Carter, Reagan staff.
- **1992-07-09** — Pool Report #28: Baker's comment on Gore ("our wives are very close friends but I'd have to wish him the worst possible luck"); Bush bilateral with Ukrainian President Kravchuk.
- **1992-07-27** — Frank Sinatra telegram (Sands Hotel, Atlantic City) to President Bush: "Do yourself a favor and ignore the blowhards…eventually they will all have laryngitis."
- **1992-09** — Bush "reassembles his crew": Jim Baker returns to the White House as Chief of Staff (trading State Dept. suit for WH suit).
- **1992-09** — Bush at 68 "attempting to defy political and actual gravity."
- **1992-11** — Letter from Bush to Condoleezza Rice in days after the November 1992 election: "I ache for your friend and mine, Brent. He has worked so hard…"
- **1992 (late)** — Bush exhorts Sig Rogich for "the Rogich creativity" (coveting Jimmy Carter's post-presidency letterhead).
- **1992 (late)** — Bush dazzles PM John Major with cutting-edge mobile phone.
- **1992 (late)** — Bush & Marlin Fitzwater hard at work, unfazed by a creature in the windowsill.
- **1992 (late)** — Bush convenes a discussion of "Noah's Ark."
- **1992 (late)** — "One very satisfying checkmark for President George HW Bush to write."

## 1993

- **1993-01** — Pool report of "The George Bush We're Outta Here World Tour" — culminating in Moscow with the signing of the START II Treaty (would have eliminated SS-18s).
- **1993-01** — In the closing days of his presidency, Bush attempts to make time to shake hands with a Treasury official named Jerome Powell.
